# Research notes

Checked 2026-11-04.

- WaterOutlook publishes no price. Its site describes "an international enterprise SaaS cloud data management platform" for operational and compliance data, says it is "100% New Zealand owned and operated", and says it collects data from "More than 3,700 users" and "Over 13,000 plants monitored" across six countries. Sources: https://www.wateroutlook.com/ and https://www.wateroutlook.com/about-us
- Its solutions page lists, under Compliance: "Continuous regulatory parameter monitoring", "Regulatory reporting" and "Automatic data feeds to/from your regulator", alongside operations, carbon, energy and KPI modules. Source: https://www.wateroutlook.com/solutions
- In May 2026 WaterOutlook launched ScheduleHub, a compliance scheduling product, timed to councils merging water services under Local Water Done Well (trade press, itbrief.co.nz).
- The rules in docs/compliance.md were read on the official sites (legislation.govt.nz, taumataarowai.govt.nz, nhmrc.gov.au, legislation.vic.gov.au, legislation.nsw.gov.au) on the same date.
